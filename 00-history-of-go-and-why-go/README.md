# 📜 History of Go & Why Go?

Before writing code, I'll start by understanding why Go exists and who built it.

## 🕰️ A Quick History
Go (or **Golang**) was started in **2007** at Google. It was created by three legendary engineers who were tired of how slow and complex other languages (like C++ and Java) had become.

### 👥 The Creators
*   **Robert Griesemer**: Helped build the V8 engine (what makes Chrome fast!).
*   **Rob Pike**: A pioneer of Unix and the creator of UTF-8.
*   **Ken Thompson**: The legend who created B, C, and Unix.

Go became **Open Source in 2009**, and the stable version (1.0) came out in **2012**.

## 🚀 Why Use Go?
Go was built for the modern world—where we have many-core processors and massive cloud networks.

1.  **Simple & Clean**: Go has very few "rules," making it easy to read and learn.
2.  **Built-in Concurrency**: It’s amazing at doing many things at once (using **Goroutines**).
3.  **Fast**: It compiles directly to machine code, so it runs at "blazing fast" speeds.
4.  **Great Tools**: It comes with everything you need to format, test, and document your code.
